## Rejected Research Results

- The first search pass found a modern Word-generated PDF about *Đạo đức cách mạng*. It was rejected because it is commentary, not the work itself.
- *Tạp chí Học tập* issue 9 from 1958 was rejected because it is not issue 12, despite its usable CC BY-NC 4.0 scan.
- Several pages about the 13 September 1958 statement used photographs of children, tree planting, or modern classrooms. They were rejected because they do not depict the stated teacher event.
- The Quân đội nhân dân article hero photograph was rejected after inspection because it shows Hồ Chí Minh with children, not the political class for teachers.
